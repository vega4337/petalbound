A field guide for small adventurers

Find your rhythm.

Move & leap

A D or ← → to move.
Space / W / ↑ to jump. Hold for height; release for a short hop.

A little burst of speed

Hold Shift to sprint. Tap it to dash after the first garden, or while powered up. Dash defeats every critter.

Grow & glow

Hit ✦ blocks from below. Pink lotus: blossom into double size and break bricks. Lavender star: 25 seconds of extra jumps and gliding.

Watch your step

Stomp blobs and flutterers. Spikes hurt from above. Jump off bounce clouds; keep moving on sinking sandbars.

Grow your abilities

First garden: permanent dash. Cloud realm: double jump. Desert realm: hold jump to glide. Story gardens save across visits.

An endless daydream

Story: four realms and a Moonkeeper finale. Endless: new patterns forever. Find three memory petals in each realm. P pause · M audio.
